Value vs close is what each pick was worth against the closing market with the vig removed. A pick at exactly the closing line at -110 scores about -4.5%; above zero means beating the close by more than the vig. It is the number to judge us by, more than win rate. Beat close: the share of picks that got a better line, or the same line at a better price, than the close.

Method

How we grade

Stamp

The model prices every QB the books post. When it finds a bet, the pick, line and price are stamped and frozen. Later runs never change them, and nothing is ever removed.

Publish

Picks go up before kickoff. Every update to this page is a public commit, so the publish time can be checked by anyone in the page's history.

Grade at the close

After the game the result comes from official stats, and the pick's line and price are compared with the closing market. Every pick, win or lose.
